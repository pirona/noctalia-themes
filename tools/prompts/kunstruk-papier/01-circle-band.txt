One large vermilion red circle placed right of centre, in front of a single heavy black horizontal band that crosses the right half of the image. Hand-pulled lithograph print on old yellowed paper: warm aged cream paper with foxing spots and darker toned edges, flat ink areas with visible lithographic grain, slight misregistration at the edges of shapes. Only two inks on the paper: deep black and vermilion red. Perfectly flat shapes with crisp edges, no shading, no relief, no shadow. Constructivist abstraction, economy of means, strong asymmetric balance, the left third of the image is calm empty paper. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border.
