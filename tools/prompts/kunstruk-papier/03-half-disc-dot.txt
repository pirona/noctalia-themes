A vermilion red half disc rising from the bottom edge on the right, like a sun on the horizon, and far above it one small solid black dot. Vast empty paper. Hand-pulled lithograph print on old yellowed paper: warm aged cream paper with foxing spots and darker toned edges, flat ink areas with visible lithographic grain, slight misregistration at the edges of shapes. Only two inks on the paper: deep black and vermilion red. Perfectly flat shapes with crisp edges, no shading, no relief, no shadow. Constructivist abstraction, economy of means, strong asymmetric balance, the left third of the image is calm empty paper. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border.
