Shapes interlocking along one 45 degree diagonal axis in the right two thirds: a thick vermilion red ring, a bone-white quarter circle, and three broad bone-white steps like a staircase, each touching the next. Deep black background. Hand-pulled lithograph print on matte paper: flat ink areas with visible lithographic grain, faint paper fibre, slight misregistration at the edges of shapes. Only three inks: deep black, vermilion red, bone white. Perfectly flat shapes with crisp edges, no shading, no relief, no shadow. Constructivist abstraction, economy of means, strong asymmetric balance, the left third of the image is calm empty black. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border.
