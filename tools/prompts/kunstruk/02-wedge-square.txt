A sharp vermilion red triangle, long and narrow like a wedge, thrusting diagonally upward from the lower right and piercing a large bone-white square tilted slightly off the vertical. Deep black background. Hand-pulled lithograph print on matte paper: flat ink areas with visible lithographic grain, faint paper fibre, slight misregistration at the edges of shapes. Only three inks: deep black, vermilion red, bone white. Constructivist abstraction, economy of means, strong asymmetric balance, the left third of the image is calm empty black. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border.
