A vermilion red half disc rising from the bottom edge on the right, like a sun on the horizon, and far above it one small solid bone-white dot. Deep black background, vast empty space. Hand-pulled lithograph print on matte paper: flat ink areas with visible lithographic grain, faint paper fibre, slight misregistration at the edges of shapes. Only three inks: deep black, vermilion red, bone white. Constructivist abstraction, economy of means, strong asymmetric balance, the left third of the image is calm empty black. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border.
