A large human head in profile facing left, built entirely from flat geometric planes and placed in the right two thirds: the face is one vermilion red plane, the back of the skull a bone-white half disc, the two halves split by a straight vertical edge, one small black square for the eye, the neck cut by the bottom edge. Deep black background. Hand-pulled lithograph print on matte paper: flat ink areas with visible lithographic grain, faint paper fibre, slight misregistration at the edges of shapes. Only three inks: deep black, vermilion red, bone white. Perfectly flat shapes with crisp edges, no shading, no relief, no shadow. Constructivist abstraction, economy of means, strong asymmetric balance, the left third of the image is calm empty black. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border.
