A white modernist building made of stacked cubic volumes with long horizontal ribbon windows, solid parapets and one cantilevered balcony, seen from a low angle and standing in the right two thirds against a deep black night sky, one vermilion red panel on its facade. Flat painted architectural poster, no railings. Hand-pulled lithograph print on matte paper: flat ink areas with visible lithographic grain, faint paper fibre, slight misregistration at the edges of shapes. Only three inks: deep black, vermilion red, bone white. Perfectly flat shapes with crisp edges, no shading, no relief, no shadow. Constructivist abstraction, economy of means, strong asymmetric balance, the left third of the image is calm empty black. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frame, no border.
