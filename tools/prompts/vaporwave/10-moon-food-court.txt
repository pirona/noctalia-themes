A huge pale full moon rests on the floor of a deserted mall food court at night, nestled between rows of empty pastel pink tables and chairs, slightly right of center, glowing with soft silvery light that casts long gentle shadows of the furniture across the glossy teal floor. Its cratered surface is rendered calmly and precisely. Behind it, the closed counters are smooth lavender walls with dark glass, and a row of slender palm trees in white planters lines the far side. Smooth, crisp, matter-of-fact painterly clarity, clean edges, flat soft shading, the impossible scale treated as perfectly ordinary, calm uncanny liminal mood, quiet dark space in the upper left, uncluttered foreground.
