An escalator in a deserted, pristine pastel shopping mall descends calmly into still turquoise sea water that fills the entire lower floor, its clean metal steps disappearing beneath the glassy surface. The upper gallery is spotless and intact: smooth lavender walls with blank rectangular panels of soft glowing light, simple neon shapes of a crescent moon, a wave and a triangle in pink and cyan light, and slender palm trees in white planters. The still water mirrors everything perfectly. Through a wide skylight, a peach and violet dusk sky. Smooth, crisp, matter-of-fact painterly clarity, clean edges, flat soft shading, the impossible flood treated as perfectly ordinary, eerie stillness, calm liminal mood, empty quiet space in the upper left, smooth water surface across the foreground.
