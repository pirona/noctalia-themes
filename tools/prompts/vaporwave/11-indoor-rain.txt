A gentle rain falls only inside a long, deserted shopping mall gallery, fine silver streaks descending from beneath a vaulted glass roof onto a glossy pale pink floor dotted with calm round puddles, while through the glass the sky above is perfectly clear, sunny and pastel blue with a few small white clouds. Smooth teal columns and closed shop fronts of lavender glass recede in perspective, potted palms stand dripping along the sides, and the puddles reflect the bright sky. Smooth, crisp, matter-of-fact painterly clarity, clean edges, flat soft shading, the impossible weather treated as perfectly ordinary, calm uncanny liminal mood, quiet open space in the upper left, uncluttered foreground.
