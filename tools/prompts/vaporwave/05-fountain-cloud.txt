In the center of a silent indoor mall plaza, a small, perfectly formed white cloud floats a few meters above a round fountain basin of pale pink marble, raining a gentle silver drizzle into the turquoise water below. Around it, a polished checkerboard floor of soft pink and teal tiles, empty benches, potted palms and a curved glass skylight showing a lavender evening sky. The cloud is rendered with the same crisp, matter-of-fact clarity as the architecture, as if it belonged there. Smooth painterly finish, clean edges, soft diffuse light, calm and uncanny liminal mood, generous quiet space in the upper left, uncluttered foreground.
