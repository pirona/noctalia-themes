An escalator in an abandoned pastel shopping mall descends calmly into still turquoise sea water that fills the entire lower floor, its metal steps disappearing beneath the glassy surface. The water perfectly mirrors the upper gallery: lavender storefront panels glowing softly, simple neon shapes of a crescent moon, a wave and a triangle in pink and cyan light, and slender palm trees in planters. Through a wide skylight, a peach and violet dusk sky. Smooth, crisp, matter-of-fact painterly clarity, clean edges, soft reflections, eerie stillness. Calm liminal mood, empty quiet space in the upper left, smooth water surface across the foreground.
