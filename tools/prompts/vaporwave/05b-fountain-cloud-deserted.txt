In the center of a completely deserted indoor mall plaza, a small, perfectly formed white cloud floats a few meters above a round fountain basin of pale pink marble, raining a gentle silver drizzle into the turquoise water below. The surrounding gallery is closed and silent: smooth lavender walls with dark, shuttered glass fronts, empty benches, potted palms and a polished checkerboard floor of soft pink and teal tiles. A curved glass skylight shows a calm lavender evening sky. The cloud is rendered with the same crisp, matter-of-fact clarity as the architecture, as if it belonged there. Smooth painterly finish, clean edges, flat soft shading, calm uncanny liminal mood, generous quiet space in the upper left, uncluttered foreground.
