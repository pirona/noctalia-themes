A long, narrow service corridor of a deserted shopping mall, pastel pink tiled walls and a row of soft fluorescent ceiling panels receding in strict one-point perspective. At the far end the corridor opens directly onto a dense, misty pine forest at dawn, tall dark green trunks and pale cyan fog, as if the building had been cut off and placed in the woods. A single potted palm stands near the right wall, its shadow falling across the glossy floor. Smooth, crisp, matter-of-fact painterly clarity, clean edges, quiet and dreamlike, the impossible doorway presented as perfectly normal. Calm liminal mood, gentle light, uncluttered foreground, empty wall space on the left.
