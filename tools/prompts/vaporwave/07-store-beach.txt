The empty top floor of a department store at dusk, rows of bare chrome clothing racks and pale pink carpet that gradually turns into soft pink sand toward the far end, where gentle turquoise waves break quietly against the base of the display windows. Warm lavender light pours in from tall windows showing a striped coral and gold sunset over the water. Small seashells rest on the carpet between the racks. Smooth, crisp, matter-of-fact painterly clarity, clean edges, the impossible transition treated as perfectly ordinary, calm uncanny liminal mood, quiet open space in the upper left, uncluttered foreground.
