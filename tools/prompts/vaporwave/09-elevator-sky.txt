The brushed steel doors of a shopping mall elevator stand wide open, slightly right of center, on a deserted upper gallery with a polished pale pink floor and smooth teal walls. Instead of a cabin, the doorway opens onto an endless sunset sky: soft coral and lavender clouds drift level with the threshold, and a striped golden sun glows low in the distance. Warm light from the sky spills out across the gallery floor in a long soft rectangle. Smooth, crisp, matter-of-fact painterly clarity, clean edges, flat soft shading, the impossible view treated as perfectly ordinary, calm uncanny liminal mood, quiet empty wall in the upper left, uncluttered foreground.
