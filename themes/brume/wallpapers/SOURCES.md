# brume wallpapers — sources

Original artwork generated locally with ComfyUI (workflow `tools/workflows/flux2_dev_wallpaper_api.json`, Flux.2 Dev fp8mixed, no LoRA, euler + Flux2Scheduler, FluxGuidance 2.0), then cropped to 1920×1080, turned grey with a fixed film grain (`ink = "grain"`) and converted to WebP. The `-pixel` ones come from the same renders, turned into fine pixel art instead (`--ink pixel`: 2 px pixels, ordered dither, 6 greys, lossless WebP).
